What it does

smartnuo has a damage calculator and a team builder, but they don't talk to each other. Fine-tuning a Pokémon's EVs meant switching back and forth between the two tabs. Speed tiers lived on another site. Every calc started with typing your own Pokémon in by hand, and saving sets and teams was limited.

Douminuo handles all of that inside smartnuo. Damage output and bulk update as you edit, with about 60 abilities and 30 items factored in. A speed table shows what each Pokémon outspeeds, and one click sends a team member to the calculator.

Problems along the way

No extra load on smartnuo's server

smartnuo is a nonprofit site its operator pays for out of pocket. From the first release, the rule was that Douminuo adds no load to its server. Every request the extension makes was audited, repeats are cached, and static data ships inside the extension. Copying a team went from up to 8 requests to none, and the first calculator run from about 100 PokéAPI calls to none.

Surviving a full site rebuild

When smartnuo moved to Nuxt 3, every feature broke at once. Version 1.3.0 switched to reading and writing the page's own state directly, and everything was back within a week.
