Trashpunk 2077

A 2D co-op multiplayer game made by a 7-person class team. Players mine resources and keep a failing space station running.

What I own

On a seven-person team, I own the space station and the mission systems: the station's resource stock and broken systems, the repair and deposit points players interact with, and the round structure that decides what the crew has to do next.

The first two rounds are set. From round three on, each round asks the crew to deliver whatever resource the station is lowest on and to repair two random systems, so the game keeps going until the crew falls behind.

How it works

The host owns the station. Clients never change it directly: depositing a resource or finishing a repair sends a request to the host, the host updates the station, and every player sees the same state and the same missions.

Where it stands

The station, repairs, deliveries and endless rounds run in multiplayer with up to four players. Mission timers and enemies are next. The game is in active development for IGME‑320 through Fall 2026.
